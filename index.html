⚠️ Important Methodology & Known Limitations

  • Scheduled Occupancy vs Physical Sensors: This application calculates occupancy purely from official timetable schedules. Ad-hoc physical meetings, sudden room cancellations, or emergency room swaps may not be reflected.
  • Vacancy Verification Criteria: A room is only marked Vacant if it exists in the verified inventory of 83 rooms and has zero overlapping scheduled sessions. Unlisted rooms are categorized as Unknown.
  • Multi-Section & Elective Cohorts: When multiple class sections attend shared lectures (e.g. PALB tracks or elective courses), all sections are displayed, flagged with a combined session tag.
  • PhD Sessions: PhD Sem II entries have unspecified room numbers in the master source; these are flagged as needs-review rather than guessing a location.
